**My own read (after yours):**

This is DreamerV3 — the headline claim is a single fixed-hyperparameter world-model agent beating tuned domain-specific baselines (PPO, Rainbow, MuZero, IMPALA, etc.) across ~150+ tasks spanning Atari, ProcGen, DMLab, continuous control, and BSuite, plus the widely-cited result of solving Minecraft diamond collection from scratch without human demonstrations or curricula. The contribution is less a new algorithmic idea than an engineering achievement in robustness — normalization, balancing, and transformations that let one config generalize across wildly different reward scales, observation types, and action spaces, which is itself notable since RL algorithms are usually brittle to exactly this kind of cross-domain transfer. One thing worth flagging: the abstract/outline don't make clear how much of the "generality" claim rests on compute/data budgets matching or exceeding baselines (e.g., R2D2+10x data, IMPALA 10x data comparisons in Fig. 1) — so the fairness of some comparisons may need checking in the body text rather than taking the headline bar charts at face value.
