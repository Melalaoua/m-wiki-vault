# Ben Landau-Taylor

# Ben Landau-Taylor

Ben Landau-Taylor is an author, researcher, and political economist who writes on historical sociology, organizational capacity, governance, and social dynamics.

## Key Concepts & Contributions
- Developed the framework of [[Social Fabric Production]], contrasting the passive consumption of community against active, logistical curation.
- Author of [[If You Build It, They Will Come]], outlining why active organizing is the ultimate shortcut to joining and leading social movements.
