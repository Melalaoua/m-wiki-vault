# Low-Stimulus Resets

[[Low-Stimulus Resets]] are offline, offline physical activities designed to let the brain recover from cognitive fatigue without exposing it to the addictive dopamine loops of the attention economy.

Unlike passive consumption (such as browsing social media, watching YouTube, or scrolling feeds), low-stimulus resets—such as gardening, washing dishes, or reading physical books—provide a tactile, slow-paced transition. They satisfy the desire for a break but are easily interrupted when mental motivation returns, helping to combat [[Cognitive Attention Degradation]].
