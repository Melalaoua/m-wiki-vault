# What is machine learning ?

Backbone of most modern AI systems, from forecasting models to autonomous vehicles to LLMs and other generative AI tools.

ML is optimizing a model's performance on a dataset of taks that adequatly resemble the real-word problems it will be used for, through a process called [model-training], the model cna make accurate predictions on the new data it sees in its ultimate use case.

In essence, machine learning aims to identify patterns within data in order to predict & act upon the real world. The deployment of an AI model is therefore called [[inference]]

[[deep-learning]] or deep artificial neural networks is SOTA in AI, it is used everywhere and implies the usage of networks of neurons
