# Cognitive Attention Degradation

[[Cognitive Attention Degradation]] is the gradual erosion of an individual's capacity for sustained deep focus. This trend is accelerated by two modern forces:

1. **Workplace Hyper-Communication**: Collaborative platforms (such as Slack) normalize constant interruption and reward rapid response times, training the brain to operate in short, fragmented bursts.
2. **Interactive AI Loops**: Engaging with hyper-responsive tools (like LLMs in [[Vibe Coding]]) reduces our tolerance for delayed gratification. This makes slow, linear cognitive processes—like reading complex papers or step-by-step mathematical derivation—feel painfully boring, leading to [[Per-Project Procrastination]] when facing hard problems.
