# Social Fabric Production

# Social Fabric Production

Social Fabric Production is the active creation, organization, and facilitation of social gatherings, rituals, and communities. It stands in contrast to a passive, consumerist attitude towards community.

## Core Principles
- **The Organizing Deficit:** There is a structural undersupply of people willing to take on the direct logistical burden of organizing, meaning that demand for social events always exceeds supply.
- **Leadership by Action:** Central nodes and leaders within social groups are almost always the individuals who put in the legwork of planning.
- **The Community Integration Shortcut:** Organizing the core activity of a target group is the fastest, highest-leverage way to integrate into a new social circle.
