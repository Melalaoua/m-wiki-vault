# It's getting harder to focus every day

Original: [[personal/raw/glyphack.com/it-s-getting-harder-to-focus-every-day]]

The article "It's getting harder to focus every day" on glyphack.com explores the modern crisis of cognitive stamina, highlighting how the author's ability to maintain deep focus has severely degraded over time. Reflecting on past habits, the author contrasts their teenage years—which involved highly intentional activities like strategic gaming, chess, and programming with minimal mobile phone interference—with the modern reality of constant distraction.

### The Erosion of Attention at Work
The transition into professional environments is identified as a major driver of attention degradation. Modern corporate communication, characterized by high Slack activity and endless meetings, rewards shallow attention and high visibility over deep work. The author notes that a high [[bullshit-ratio]] at work allows individuals who only give 10% of their actual attention to succeed, creating toxic habits that bleed into personal programming and learning goals.

### The LLM Feedback Loop
While Large Language Models (LLMs) are highly productive, they introduce severe cognitive side effects:
1. **Attention Fragmentation**: Outsourcing tasks to LLMs encourages a fractured "research mode" where a user attempts to run multiple processes simultaneously while waiting for generations.
2. **Overstimulation**: The hyper-fast feedback of [[vibe-coding]] with AI makes traditional, slower cognitive tasks (like reading a research paper or thoroughly debugging a codebase) feel boring and frustrating.

### Mitigation Strategies
To fight back against [[cognitive-attention-degradation]], the author employs several strategies:
* **Artificial Friction & Accountability**: Using short 15-minute timers to lower the barrier to entry, or live-streaming coding sessions to prevent picking up the phone.
* **[[low-stimulus-resets]]**: Utilizing offline, analog hobbies like balcony gardening or reading physical books to let the brain rest without sliding into highly addictive passive consumption.

## Key claims

- The author struggles to maintain focus for more than 10 minutes at a time without getting pulled away by low-friction distractions. ([[personal/raw/glyphack.com/it-s-getting-harder-to-focus-every-day#It's getting harder to focus every day]]) — "I spend 10 minute on something then I get distracted, and try to focus again."
- The professional workplace environment incentivizes shallow focus, allowing individuals to succeed with minimal deep attention. ([[personal/raw/glyphack.com/it-s-getting-harder-to-focus-every-day#The first time I saw people not putting the effort and still get the reward for it was at work]]) — "As long as people give 10% of their attention to work they are considered fine in most environments."
- The rapid pace and instant results of working with LLMs makes slower, traditional workflows feel boring and hard to focus on. ([[personal/raw/glyphack.com/it-s-getting-harder-to-focus-every-day#The next big change is more usage of LLMs]]) — "And this is overstimulating, in a way that doing something without LLM sometimes is boring. You don't see the results as fast."
